# Isotherm Applied Sciences Inc.

**Deterministic systems for continuity, provenance, and trust.**

Isotherm Applied Sciences is an independent engineering studio based in Ottawa, Canada.  
We build transparent, minimal-systems software for identity, verification, provenance, and continuity workflows.  
Our tools support collectors, archivists, institutions, and developers who require clarity, authenticity, and long‑term trust across digital and physical ecosystems.

We design systems that behave deterministically and integrate cleanly into larger workflows.

---

## Mission

To create open‑source tools that strengthen authenticity, provenance integrity, and continuity across ecosystems.  
Our engineering philosophy emphasizes clarity, auditability, and deterministic behavior.
