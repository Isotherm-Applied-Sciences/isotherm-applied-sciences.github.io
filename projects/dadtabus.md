# Dadtabus

Dadtabus is a universal identity continuity substrate designed to unify identity, provenance, and verification across systems.  
It provides a deterministic foundation for long‑term trust, continuity, and cross‑ecosystem identity workflows.

## Status
Active development — conceptual architecture and substrate model in progress.

## Roadmap
- Identity continuity substrate
- Provenance and verification integration
- CollectorCheck interoperability
- Continuity ledger foundation
- Multi‑ecosystem identity mapping

## Purpose
Dadtabus is the identity backbone of the Isotherm continuity stack.  
It enables deterministic identity workflows across digital and physical systems.
