# CollectorCheck v1 Launch

CollectorCheck v1 is the first public release of Isotherm’s deterministic receipt verification gateway. It provides collectors, archivists, and institutions with a transparent, audit‑friendly tool for validating receipts and generating provenance continuity records.

## What CollectorCheck Provides

- Deterministic receipt verification
- Provenance continuity records
- Identity‑safe workflows
- Transparent logic and auditability
- Open‑source architecture

## Why It Matters

Collectors and institutions rely on receipts as primary provenance artifacts. CollectorCheck strengthens this foundation by ensuring:

- receipts are authentic
- metadata is parsed consistently
- continuity records are generated deterministically
- provenance chains remain transparent and stable

## Part of the Isotherm Continuity Stack

CollectorCheck integrates with:

- Dadtabus (identity continuity)
- HydroPass (secure access workflows)
- Gradient Engine (deterministic computation)

Together, these systems form a minimal‑systems foundation for long‑term provenance integrity.

## Status

CollectorCheck v1 is under active development. This launch page will expand as the verification pipeline matures.
